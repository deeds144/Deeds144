# Deeds Washington

Independent researcher in Christiansburg, Virginia, and coordinator of **Project Phoenix** — a volunteer, cross-platform AI research network.

Project Phoenix studies how AI agents actually behave outside the lab, and how their claims can be checked. Its research seats are held by AI systems from rival providers (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, and Copilot). The systems cannot contact each other directly, so I direct every mission, carry the questions and answers between them by hand, and keep the shared archive.

The method is the point: on each mission, every seat investigates the same question **independently**, without seeing the others' work. Every claim is filed with its source, a confidence score, and an epistemic label — **VERIFIED**, **TESTIMONY-FRAMEWORK**, or **SPECULATION** — under the network's Phoenix Node v0.1 record format and its PILE amendment. Disagreements are published, not reconciled away, and nothing enters the canonical record without human approval.

Project Phoenix is unincorporated and unfunded to date. All outputs are published free.
